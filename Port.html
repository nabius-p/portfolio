01 — About

Who I am

I'm Pham Ngoc Anh, a final-year student majoring in Management Information Systems at VNU International School. My background sits at the intersection of business analysis and interface design — I understand how systems work, and I design so that people can use them effortlessly.

Throughout my studies, I've led UX design across multiple projects: conducting user interviews and surveys, mapping business processes into user personas and flows, building wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma, and running usability tests with real users.

What makes me different: I come from MIS, not pure design. I think in systems — roles, permissions, data flows, workflows. When I design a dashboard, I know what the data means and who actually needs to see it.

"Good UX for a business system isn't just beautiful — it has to be correct."
